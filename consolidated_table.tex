Residual Printing Leakage, True Repayment & Mitigation

| Category                              | Factual Element                                      | Quantitative / Empirical Content                          | Source / Foundation                  |
|---------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Core Definition                   | Residual Printing Leakage                           | Continuous stock of issued claims never retired against real output | Model definition (locked)           |
| Debt Dynamics Identity            | Change in debt-to-GDP                               | \(\Delta d_t \approx (r_t - g_t) d_{t-1} - pb_t\)          | CBO, IMF, Treasury, academic standard |
| True Repayment Channel            | Primary surplus (\(pb_t > 0\))                      | Only pure “repayment in kind” mechanism                   | Standard public finance             |
| Modern-Era True Repayment Fraction| \(\rho_t\) (share actually extinguished)            | Typically 0.10 – 0.25 (often 0.00 – 0.15 in deficit years) | Historical primary balance data     |
| Never-Repaid Fraction (Best Case) | Post-WWII U.S. (1946–1974)                          | 0.60 – 0.80 of the debt-ratio reduction                   | Hall & Sargent; Acalin & Ball; related decompositions |
| Mitigation Channels               | Growth differential                                 | \(\max(0, g_t - r_t) \cdot D_{t-1}\)                      | Debt dynamics identity              |
|                                       | Inflation / Financial repression                    | \(\kappa \cdot \pi_t \cdot D_{t-1}\)                      | Reinhart & Sbrancia; historical evidence |
| Effective Residual Addition       | Period \(t\) formula                                | \(\Delta R_t = C_t (1 - \rho_t) - M_t\)                   | Derived from above                  |
| Cumulative Non-Repaid Fraction    | Over horizon \([1,T]\)                              | \(1 - \dfrac{\sum pb_t}{\lvert d_T - d_0 \rvert}\)        | Integrated formulation              |
| Current Regime Implication        | Structural primary deficits                         | High permanent residual fraction (≈ 75–90% of new claims) | Post-1980s / post-2008 data         |
