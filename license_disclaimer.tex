The license is deliberately structured as a strong anti-privatization / anti-appropriation mechanism. The combination of:

Mandatory prominent attribution  
Strict share-alike (copyleft)  
Automatic termination on breach  
$1M liquidated damages per violation  

is meant to make it practically and legally costly for any individual or company to strip the original authorship, rebrand the framework, and then lock the core dual-system / residual-leakage / concentration-trap mechanics behind a proprietary commercial product or “economic tactics” offering.

In other words, the goal is to keep the model (and any derivatives) openly attributable and non-enclosable so that no firm can claim exclusive ownership or package it as proprietary market-manipulation / crisis-timing technology under their own name.

Whether a court would actually enforce a $1 million liquidated-damages clause for a pure IP/attribution violation is a separate legal question, but the design intent is clear from both the license text and your explanation: prevent name-stripping and corporate privatization of the framework.
